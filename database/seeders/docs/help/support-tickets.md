Support tickets are the fastest way to reach the right DeveloperJet team, and keep a full history of the conversation in your dashboard.

## Open a ticket

1. Go to **Dashboard → Tickets** and choose **Open a ticket** (or go to `/dashboard/tickets/new`).
2. Choose a **Department**:

   | Department | Use it for |
   | --- | --- |
   | General | Anything that does not fit elsewhere |
   | Billing | Payments, invoices, wallet, renewals, refunds |
   | Technical Support | Access, setup, errors on your service |
   | Sales | Plans, quotes and pre-sales questions |
   | Development | Professional development projects |
   | SEO | SEO projects and reports |

3. Choose a **Priority**: *Low*, *Normal*, *High* or *Urgent*. Use *Urgent* only when a live service is down.
4. Choose the **Related service (optional)** so our team sees the server and plan straight away.
5. Write a clear **Subject** (up to 160 characters) and **Message** (up to 8,000 characters).
6. Optionally add **Attachments**: up to 3 files, each up to 5 MB, in `jpg`, `jpeg`, `png`, `pdf`, `txt` or `zip` format.
7. Press **Submit ticket**.

## What to include

- The order number or service name.
- What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
- The exact error message, or a screenshot.
- What you have already tried.
- For payment questions, the payment reference (UTR / payment ID).

> **Warning:** Never include passwords, one-time codes, private SSH keys or API keys in a ticket. We will never ask for them. Public SSH keys (`.pub`) are fine.

## Follow and reply

- Replies appear on the ticket page and are also sent to your email.
- Use **Reply** at the bottom of the ticket, then **Send reply**. You can attach files to a reply.
- Keep one topic per ticket so it reaches the right person.

## Close a ticket

When your issue is resolved, press **Close ticket**. If the problem returns, open a new ticket and mention the old ticket number.

## Before you open a ticket

Many answers are in [Common errors and troubleshooting](/docs/common-errors). Searching the documentation often gets you an answer immediately.

## Troubleshooting

- **The file will not upload.** Check the type and size limits above. Compress large logs into a `.zip`.
- **I cannot open the Tickets page.** You need a verified account. See [Email verification](/docs/email-verification).
