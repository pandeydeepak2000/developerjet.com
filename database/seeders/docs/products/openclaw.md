OpenClaw is a personal AI assistant. Each customer gets an isolated deployment on a VPS used only by them, with gateway authentication and an isolated service user.

> **Note:** OpenClaw plan details are still being finalised. Plans that are not yet approved show *Pricing to be confirmed* and cannot be bought online.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants a private assistant without managing a server. No server skills are needed; you should know how to create an API key with an AI provider.

## What is included

- A VPS assigned to your order and used only by you.
- OpenClaw installed behind authenticated access.
- Access instructions through your dashboard.

**Not included:** AI model usage (bring your own key), root access and custom integrations.

## Getting started

1. Order an OpenClaw plan and wait for the service to become **Active**.
2. Add your AI provider key on the service page. See [API provider configuration](/docs/api-provider-configuration).
3. Open OpenClaw through the secured gateway using the details on your service page.

## Custom domain

OpenClaw supports connecting a domain you already own. See [Custom domain setup](/docs/custom-domain-setup).

## Privacy

Your deployment is isolated from other customers. Your provider key is encrypted and write-only. Conversations are processed by the AI provider whose key you supply, under that provider's terms.

## Troubleshooting

- **The gateway asks for credentials I do not have.** They are on your service page. If not, open a Technical Support ticket — we never send credentials by email.
- **The assistant does not respond.** Check your provider key and your provider account's balance or limits.
