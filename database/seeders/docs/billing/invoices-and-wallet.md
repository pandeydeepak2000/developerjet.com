## Invoices

An invoice is issued **only after a payment is verified**. Unpaid orders do not have invoices.

### Download an invoice

1. Go to **Dashboard → Invoices**.
2. Find the invoice by number, date or amount.
3. Select **PDF** to download the invoice.

You can also open an order and see its invoices in the **Invoices** panel.

### What appears on an invoice

- The **billing name**, **billing address** and optional **GSTIN** you entered at checkout.
- Each plan and add-on, the billing period, any discount, tax (when configured) and the total.
- DeveloperJet's company details.

> **Tip:** Enter your billing details carefully at checkout. To correct an issued invoice, open a **Billing** ticket — invoices are corrected with a credit note rather than edited.

## Wallet

Your wallet holds prepaid funds in Indian rupees that you can apply at checkout.

### See your balance

Go to **Dashboard → Wallet**. You see the balance and a ledger of every credit and debit, including funds reserved for unpaid orders and funds returned when an order is cancelled.

### Add funds (top-up request)

Top-ups are verified by our team before funds are credited:

1. Pay the amount using the details provided by our Sales team.
2. Go to **Dashboard → Wallet**.
3. Enter the **Amount (₹)** — between ₹1 and ₹10,00,000.
4. Add the **Payment reference (optional)** — for example the UTR or transaction ID — and a **Note (optional)**.
5. Press **Submit top-up request**.
6. The request appears under **Top-up requests**. When our team has independently confirmed the funds, your wallet is credited.

> **Warning:** Submitting a request does not add money by itself. Funds are credited only after the payment is verified.

### Using the wallet at checkout

Tick **Apply wallet balance** on the checkout page. See [Payment methods](/docs/payment-methods) for how full and partial wallet payments work.

## Troubleshooting

- **My top-up is still pending.** Verification is manual. Make sure you added a payment reference; if it has been more than one working day, open a Billing ticket.
- **I cannot find an invoice.** The order may not be paid yet. Check the order status in **Dashboard → Orders**.
