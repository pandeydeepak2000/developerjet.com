DeveloperJet runs AI agents, automation tools and developer environments on a VPS assigned to you. This guide walks through the whole journey once, so you know what happens at each step and where to find more detail.

## Before you begin

You need:

- An email address you can access — we send a one-time verification code to it.
- A payment method accepted at checkout, or funds in your DeveloperJet wallet.
- For AI products, an API key from your own AI provider account. You add it on your server after delivery.
- For a custom address on supported apps, access to the DNS settings of a domain you already own. **DeveloperJet does not sell, register or transfer domains.**

## The journey at a glance

[diagram: order-flow]

1. **Create an account** at **Register** and verify your email with the 6-digit code. See [Account registration](/docs/account-registration) and [Email verification](/docs/email-verification).
2. **Choose a plan.** Compare products on **Pricing** or a product page. Only plans with an approved price can be ordered online. See [Plan selection](/docs/plan-selection).
3. **Add it to your cart and check out.** Checkout confirms that the server is handed over with full root access and how the order is delivered. See [Cart and checkout](/docs/cart-and-checkout).
4. **Pay.** Your payment is confirmed on our servers — never just by returning from a payment page. See [Payment methods](/docs/payment-methods).
5. **Wait for setup.** Your order shows **Awaiting VPS assignment** while our team adds a server, then your software is installed automatically. Delivery is not instant. See [How orders are delivered](/docs/how-orders-are-delivered).
6. **Log in.** When the service is active, the **Login & SSH** tab of the service panel shows your IP, username and root password. See [Service access](/docs/service-access).
7. **Configure it.** Add your AI provider key with the command shown on the service Overview and, for web apps, connect your own domain if you want one. See [API provider configuration](/docs/api-provider-configuration) and [Custom domain setup](/docs/custom-domain-setup).

> **Note:** Your invoice is issued only after the payment is verified, and appears under **Invoices** in your dashboard.

## Good habits from day one

- Never share your one-time code, password or API keys — DeveloperJet staff will never ask for them.
- Keep your email address current: reminders, renewal notices and ticket replies are sent there.
- Read the [Billing and renewals](/docs/billing-and-renewals) guide so a renewal never catches you by surprise.

## Troubleshooting

- **No verification email?** Check spam, wait a minute and use **Resend**. See [Common errors](/docs/common-errors).
- **A plan shows "Pricing to be confirmed"?** It cannot be bought online yet. Contact Sales or request a quote.
- **Paid but the order still says Pending payment?** Payments are reconciled automatically every few minutes. If it does not update, open a ticket with your payment reference.
