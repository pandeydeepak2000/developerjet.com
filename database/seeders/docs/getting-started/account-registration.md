Your DeveloperJet account holds your orders, services, invoices, wallet and support tickets. Registration takes about two minutes.

## Step-by-step

1. Choose **Get Started** in the site header, or go to `/register`.
2. Fill in **Your name**, **Email address**, **Create a password** and **Confirm password**.
3. Press **Create account**.
4. You are signed in straight away and taken to the **Verify your email** page.
5. Open the email titled *Your DeveloperJet verification code*, enter the **6-digit code** and press **Verify email**. Your account is now verified.

> **Tip:** Use an email address you check regularly and that your team can access if the account belongs to a business. Renewal reminders and ticket replies are sent there.

## Choosing a strong password

- Use a long passphrase — at least 12 characters is a good rule — or a password manager.
- Do not reuse a password from another site.
- Your password is stored only as a secure hash; nobody at DeveloperJet can see it.

## What an unverified account can do

| Action | Unverified | Verified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browse plans and documentation | Yes | Yes |
| Add plans to the cart | Yes | Yes |
| Check out and pay | No | Yes |
| Open the customer dashboard | No | Yes |
| Open support tickets | No | Yes |

If you try to open a verified-only page, you are sent back to the verification page.

## Guest cart

You can add plans to your cart before you register. When you sign in, the items in your guest cart are merged into your account cart automatically.

## Troubleshooting

- **"The email has already been taken"** — an account already exists for that address. Use **Log in**, or **Forgot password?** if you cannot remember the password.
- **Too many attempts** — registration is rate-limited to protect the service. Wait a minute and try again.
- **No code arrived** — see [Email verification](/docs/email-verification).
