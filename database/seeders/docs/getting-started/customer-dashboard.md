The customer dashboard at `/dashboard` is where you manage everything after you sign in. It is available once your email is verified.

## Overview

The landing page shows summary cards for **Orders**, **Services**, **Invoices** and **Wallet balance**. Select a card to open that section.

## Sections

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Orders** | See every order and its status, pay an unpaid order, cancel an unpaid order. |
| **Services** | Open a service to see its server, status, renewal date, controls and deployment progress; add an AI provider key; connect a custom domain on supported apps. |
| **Requests** | Track quote requests for professional services and request-access products, and accept or decline a quote. |
| **Projects** | Follow professional-service projects: milestones, messages, files and reports. |
| **Wallet** | See your balance and history, and request a top-up. |
| **Invoices** | Download PDF invoices for verified payments. |
| **Tickets** | Open and follow support tickets. |

## Order statuses explained

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending payment | The order is created but not paid yet. You can pay or cancel it. |
| Paid | Payment has been verified on our servers. |
| Awaiting VPS assignment | Our team is assigning a server to your order. This is a manual step. |
| Provisioning | Your software is being installed and checked. |
| Active | Your service is ready. Access details are on the service page. |
| Failed | A payment did not complete. Nothing was charged for a failed payment. |
| Cancelled | The order was cancelled before payment. |
| Refund pending / Refunded | A refund is being processed or has been completed. |

## Service page

Open **Services** and select a service to see:

- **Overview** — plan, order, resources, service URL (when active) and renewal date.
- **Server** — server status, IP address (shown when active), region, resources, operating system and the access model.
- **Controls** — the server controls that are verified for your server. Controls that are not available yet are listed with a reason, so you know what to expect.
- **Deployment progress** — whether your service is queued, being set up, or needs a retry by our team.
- **AI provider** — add or replace your provider key (supported products).
- **Domain** — connect an existing domain (supported products).

> **Note:** Usage metrics are not available yet. Backup details are shared once your service is configured.
